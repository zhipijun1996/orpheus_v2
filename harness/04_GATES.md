# Gates

## Story Gate

### Human Motive
- desire is understandable in ordinary language
- the character possesses sufficient knowledge for the action
- major reversals are grounded in experience strong enough to alter the prior commitment
- the character remains an agent rather than a carrier of required plot information

### Drama Proof
- explicit desire and incompatibility
- protagonist action changes the situation
- at least 2 concrete signature scenes
- ending changes a relationship, life, or durable commitment
- Logic Scout is not `DEAD`

## Reveal Gate
- Working Truth is credible and actionable
- Deep Truth creates meaningful Reveal Delta
- revealed evidence remains fair
- at least one concrete scene carries the reveal
- reveal does not depend on a failed character motive

## Prototype Gate
- 6–12 beats read as one causal story
- character action remains legible without technical explanation
- Cold Reader identifies at least one strong hook
- Drama Review cites actual scene evidence

## Integration Gate
- each active Route has a current Interface
- shared facts satisfy required common inputs
- required-open facts remain open
- collisions are resolved, deferred explicitly, or escalated
- shared additions have a clear cross-route function

## Closure Gate
`PLAUSIBLE -> SOLVABLE -> PROVEN`

## Human Gate
Human Pack:
- 1–3 candidates
- one spine each
- largest difference
- largest risk
- strongest objection
- one decision only
